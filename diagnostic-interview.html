    Before the confidential interview

    Your perspective will inform an organizational finding—not an individual evaluation.

    The sponsor can see

    Whether your interview is complete and whether the diagnostic has enough participation to proceed.

    The sponsor cannot see

    Your individual answers, interview record, adaptive follow-ups, or attributed quotations.

    The platform does

    Review evidence quality, de-identify operating examples, and synthesize themes across informed perspectives.

    The platform does not

    Guarantee anonymity, rank participants, diagnose individuals, or treat one answer as organizational truth.
