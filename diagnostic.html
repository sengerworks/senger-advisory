The Diagnostic

See the organization clearly before choosing what to change.

Most organizations reach for interventions too quickly: reorganization, training, AI, coaching, new tools, new meetings. The diagnostic applies the Capacity Lens to reveal the system beneath the symptoms before leaders commit to a solution.

What it reveals

The diagnostic turns the Capacity Lens into a defensible organizational hypothesis.

The free assessment suggests what an organization may be experiencing. The paid diagnostic investigates why: it gathers confidential evidence across informed perspectives, tests competing explanations, and connects the strongest constraint hypothesis to a recommended intervention.

Method

One rigorous method, delivered with automation or expert guidance.

01

Discovery

Clarify the execution demand, changing complexity, triggering concern, and decisions currently at risk.

02

Participant design

Select informed perspectives and identify coverage gaps before evidence collection begins.

03

Guided interviews

Use one contextualized question protocol to gather confidential operating examples from every participant.

04

Evidence synthesis

De-identify responses and examine convergence, meaningful variation, contradictions, and blind spots.

05

Capacity finding

Develop the strongest constraint hypothesis, show the supporting evidence, and test competing explanations.

06

Focused intervention

Translate the finding into an appropriately scaled intervention, evidence plan, and reassessment path.

Two delivery routes

The same diagnostic standard. A different level of facilitation.

01

Automated diagnostic

A confidential, asynchronous guided interview experience with adaptive follow-ups, evidence-quality checks, structured synthesis, and human escalation when needed.

02

Advisor-led diagnostic

Live discovery and interviews, skilled human probing, advisor-reviewed synthesis, facilitated interpretation, and custom intervention design.
