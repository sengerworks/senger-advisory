The Diagnostic

Diagnose capacity before prescribing change.

Most organizations reach for interventions too quickly: reorganization, training, AI, coaching, new tools, new meetings. The diagnostic slows the instinct to prescribe and identifies where capacity is actually constrained.

What it reveals

The diagnostic turns execution drag into visible capacity signals.

It is designed for growing companies, business units, and leadership teams that have momentum but feel increasing friction in how work moves.
