Moderate confidence
What did we diagnose?

Routine decision ownership collapses upward under ambiguity.

Authority and information expectations have not evolved with cross-functional complexity. Senior attention is increasingly used to route work that should move closer to execution.

Why it matters now

Leadership attention is becoming the limiting resource.

A planned market expansion will increase the number and consequence of decisions crossing functional boundaries.

Why we believe it

Multiple perspectives support the pattern.

Approved evidence shows repeated escalation, while variation preserves information readiness as a credible competing explanation.

What did we decide?

Test authority before adding structure.

Clarify authority and minimum information standards for three recurring decision types over 90 days.

What needs attention?

Information quality may be the stronger constraint.

The 30-day review must distinguish authority friction from late or incomplete operating information.
