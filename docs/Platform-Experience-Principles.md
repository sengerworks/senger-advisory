# Platform Experience Principles

These principles govern every role-based workflow in the Organizational Capacity Platform.

## Orient, act, confirm

1. **Orient:** A workspace home explains the purpose, journey, privacy boundary, current status,
   and next action. It is not the place where the work itself is performed.
2. **Act:** Every meaningful task opens in a focused route with one primary objective per screen.
3. **Confirm:** Completion produces a plain-language confirmation, the resulting state, and the
   next available action.

## Guided-process standard

- Every multi-step process has a named stage, visible progress, and clear Back and Continue actions.
- Moving backward preserves prior entries and permits correction.
- Approved records are saved server-side and remain available across sessions and devices.
- Unapproved drafts must become server-recoverable before a workflow is considered production-complete.
- A role sees only the controls, language, and evidence appropriate to that role.
- Progressive disclosure keeps methodology and support available without competing with the current task.
- The same interaction grammar applies to sponsor, participant, steward, and platform-operator journeys.

## Sponsor diagnostic journey

The six top-level stages are: Frame the Moment, Choose the Perspectives, Approve the Protocol,
Send Invitations, Follow Participation, and Review the Finding. Each stage may contain a short
sequence of focused screens, but stage changes always produce a distinct, recoverable route.
