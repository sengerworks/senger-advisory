# POC Orientation and Participant Terms

Version: 2.0.0

The POC opens with method orientation followed by an explicit participation acknowledgement. The
orientation gives sponsors and participants a shared vocabulary without teaching them to diagnose
the organization or favor a particular explanation.

## Method orientation

- **Organizational capacity:** the organization’s ability to carry its current complexity and
  commitments without disproportionate friction or unsustainable effort.
- **Constraint:** a condition that disproportionately limits execution.
- **Friction:** observable drag as decisions, information, resources, or work move.
- **Capacity Compensation:** heroics, escalation, meetings, workarounds, or manual coordination that
  preserve performance while concealing strain.

The five inquiry lenses are Priority & Attention, Authority & Accountability, Information &
Sensemaking, Coordination, and Resource & Capability Deployment. They organize inquiry; they are
not scores, conclusions, or assumptions that a constraint exists.

## Participant acknowledgement

Before interview access, the participant is told that participation is voluntary and examines the
organizational system rather than individual performance; operational identity records are separate
from interview content; the sponsor sees status but not answers, attributed excerpts, or individual
scores; organizational findings remain withheld below five completed perspectives and until all
review gates pass; approved de-identified evidence may receive AI-assisted processing subject to
human disclosure and advisor review; the POC ends at a governed finding and Intervention
Directions; and support and optional product feedback remain outside diagnostic evidence.

Acceptance records only the version and timestamp on the identity-side participant slot. It does not
copy the acknowledgement into interview content. Version 2.0.0 supersedes the narrower 1.0.0 notice,
so a participant whose slot carries the older version must review and accept the current terms.

The public privacy summary at `privacy.html#diagnostic-participation` explains these boundaries in
more detail. This product acknowledgement should receive qualified legal review before broad paid
launch or use in jurisdictions requiring additional employment, privacy, AI, or works-council terms.
