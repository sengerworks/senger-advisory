# Presentation Narrative v0.1

## Opening recognition
Most leaders in growing organizations know the feeling: the strategy is clear, the people are capable, and yet execution feels heavier than it should.

## Core insight
The organization you built to reach this stage may not be the organization that gets you to the next one.

## Thesis
Growth creates complexity faster than organizational capacity evolves.

## Narrative arc
1. Growth creates new coordination demands.
2. Coordination creates complexity.
3. Complexity creates friction.
4. Friction creates value leakage.
5. Value leakage slows execution.
6. Leaders often misread the symptoms as people, strategy, or technology problems.
7. The better first move is diagnosis.

## Executive takeaway
Do not ask first: What intervention do we need? Ask: Where is our capacity constrained?

## Closing line
Measure the capacity your strategy depends on.
