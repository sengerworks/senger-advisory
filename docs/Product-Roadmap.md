## Phase 4 — Organizational diagnostic platform
- Establish the free assessment as a lightweight, repeatable signal gauge and conversion path,
  not the organizational diagnostic
- Define a governed diagnostic method spanning discovery, participant design, a contextualized
  common question protocol, confidential responses, de-identification, synthesis, confidence,
  reporting, and intervention recommendation
- Add perspective-coverage guidance and sponsor-bias safeguards to participant selection
- Build confidential asynchronous guided diagnostic interviews with adaptive written
  follow-ups for the automated route
- Support live interview, recording, transcript, de-identification, and human-review workflows
  for the advisor-led route
- Add evidence traceability, competing explanations, contradictions, blind spots, diagnostic
  confidence, retention, deletion, and human-escalation rules
- Produce one versioned diagnostic record and recommended intervention across both routes

## Phase 5 — Capacity Operating Brief and guided intervention
- Replace the generic dashboard concept with a living executive Capacity Operating Brief
- Center the Brief on the diagnosed constraint, supporting evidence, accepted intervention,
  expected operating change, decisions requiring attention, and next review
- Separate intervention delivery, observed operating change, and reassessed capacity change
- Add Diagnostic Record, Intervention Plan, Evidence Journal, Learning Path, Reassessment, and
  Decision History views with progressive disclosure
- Add platform-guided intervention options, 30/60/90-day action paths, just-in-time learning,
  templates, evidence prompts, check-ins, and escalation signals
- Give automated and advisor-led customers the same client-facing Brief while providing
  advisors additional governed facilitation and intervention controls
- Let customers escalate from automated to advisor-led service without losing diagnostic,
  intervention, evidence, decision, or reassessment history
- Govern automated recommendations as testable hypotheses rather than prescriptions or causal
  conclusions

## Phase 6 — Co-branded partner platform
- Add a platform → partner → client-organization tenancy hierarchy with strict cross-client
  isolation
- Support governed partner branding across invitations, assessment surfaces, reports,
  learning, and workspaces
- Add partner-specific advisor identity, contact, offers, escalation, and scheduling paths
- Add partner administration, subscriptions, licensing, usage, and billing controls
- Support approved partner-authored learning and intervention assets with provenance and
  audit history
- Define Senger Advisory attribution, methodology/version control, data ownership, retention,
  and partner quality standards

## Final-product destination
The Organizational Capacity Platform becomes a co-brandable operating system that moves a
customer from a free capacity signal to a paid diagnosis, appropriately scaled intervention,
and verified learning. Automated and advisor-led routes use one governed diagnostic method
and produce the same client-facing Capacity Operating Brief. The platform keeps one versioned
diagnostic, privacy, evidence, action-cycle, decision, and reassessment foundation. The
canonical architecture and commercial model are documented in
`docs/Product-Architecture-and-Vision.md`.
