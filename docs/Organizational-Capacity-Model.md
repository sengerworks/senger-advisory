# Organizational Capacity Model v0.1

## Core Thesis
Growth creates complexity faster than organizational capacity evolves.

## Definition
Organizational Capacity is the ability of an organization to absorb complexity and convert strategy into coordinated execution without excessive friction, value leakage, or leadership overload.

## The Model
Business Strategy -> Leadership System -> Organizational Capacity -> Execution Flow -> Business Outcomes.

## Six Capacity Domains
1. Leadership
2. Decision Velocity
3. Operating Rhythm
4. Alignment
5. Technology
6. Culture

## Core Insight
The organization you built to reach this stage may not be the organization that gets you to the next one.
