# POC Client Enrollment Protocol

Use this protocol to enroll each of the first five Organizational Capacity Platform POC clients.
It creates a consistent launch without configuring the diagnostic around a presumed finding.

## Do not create a workspace until

- The organization has explicitly agreed to participate in the POC.
- One accountable sponsor has confirmed the email address they will use.
- The sponsor understands that they provide context but do not consume a participant slot.
- The sponsor agrees to invite five to ten relevant internal perspectives. Participants may be
  executives, managers, individual contributors, peers, or cross-functional colleagues.
- The confidentiality model, minimum release threshold of five, and de-identification boundaries
  have been explained.
- The sponsor understands that the POC releases a governed finding and prioritized Intervention
  Directions, then stops before detailed design or delivery.
- A target completion window and final debrief have been agreed.

## Provisioning sequence

1. Open Platform Operations at `https://sengeradvisory.com/workspace/operations.html`.
2. Enter the confirmed organization name and sponsor email.
3. Select the automated written or advisor-led diagnostic route.
4. Confirm the scope and confidentiality acknowledgements.
5. Create the POC once. If the request fails, retry the unchanged enrollment; the provisioning
   receipt safely resumes the same organization, workspace, diagnostic, and invitation.
6. Confirm that the new organization becomes active, a no-charge POC diagnostic appears, and the
   sponsor invitation has an expiration date.
7. Assign the verified advisor Clerk user ID for the agreed review period.
8. Record the client only in the authorized operating system. Do not place sponsor emails,
   participant identities, or confidential organizational context in this repository.

## Enrollment success

An engagement is ready for sponsor discovery only when its isolated workspace and POC entitlement
exist, the sponsor can authenticate through the canonical POC destination, and a bounded advisor
assignment is active. Client context, diagnostic questions, findings, and Intervention Directions
must remain empty until the governed workflow produces them.
