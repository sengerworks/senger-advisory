# How Senger Advisory Protects Diagnostic Information

The Organizational Capacity Platform is designed to help leaders understand organizational patterns without exposing individual participants. Confidentiality is part of the diagnostic method, not an optional setting.

## What leaders can see

Authorized client leaders can see diagnostic progress, participation totals, evidence-based organizational themes, uncertainty, the validated constraint hypothesis, and the agreed intervention. They do not receive individual answers, attributed quotations, or participant-level scores.

## How participant information is protected

- Identity and participation records are separated from confidential interview content.
- Raw responses do not contain participant names, email addresses, or account identifiers.
- Evidence is de-identified and reviewed for disclosure risk before synthesis.
- High-risk material requires human review and may be paraphrased, generalized, restricted, or excluded.
- Findings remain blocked until participation and confidentiality gates are satisfied.
- Access is limited by workspace, role, purpose, and time-bounded advisor assignment.
- Material actions are recorded for auditability.

## How AI is used

AI supports bounded evidence preparation and synthesis. It receives approved de-identified evidence, not participant identity or payment information. The configured synthesis request disables provider storage. AI output remains a draft until an authorized advisor reviews it, and client leadership has a separate validation step before an intervention advances.

## An honest confidentiality commitment

De-identification substantially reduces risk but cannot guarantee that every distinctive event or perspective is impossible to recognize. Senger Advisory therefore combines removal of direct identifiers with cohort protections, disclosure-risk review, quotation controls, aggregation, and human judgment.

## Production assurance

Before production launch, Senger Advisory will publish applicable subprocessors, retention and deletion terms, an incident-response contact, infrastructure assurances, and independent security-testing status. Certifications will be stated only after they have been earned.
