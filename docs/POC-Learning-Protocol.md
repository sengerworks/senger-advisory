# POC Learning Protocol

## Purpose

Each POC must produce two distinct outputs: a governed organizational result for the client and operational learning for Senger Advisory. Product feedback never becomes diagnostic evidence, alters a finding, or exposes participant identity or interview content.

## Checkpoint sequence

1. **Sponsor setup** — After context approval and participant design, capture whether the workflow was clear and where staff support was required.
2. **Participant interview completion** — Offer optional, identity-free feedback immediately after submission. Declining feedback never affects participation or the diagnostic.
3. **Finding release** — When the sponsor reviews the governed finding, capture clarity, actionability, trust, friction, and the perceived value of a paid next step.
4. **Final debrief** — At the Intervention Directions boundary, capture the sponsor's overall experience and purchase intent without delivering the paid intervention.
5. **Advisor closeout** — Review the aggregate scorecard, operational support cases, and checkpoint coverage. Record one immutable cohort decision: continue, adjust, hold, narrow scope, or ready to monetize.
6. **Change governance** — Convert recurring or material friction into a change candidate with an owner, severity, validation measure, rollback condition, and explicit decision. A change is not complete until its validation result is recorded.

## Ownership and timing

- The sponsor owns optional sponsor feedback.
- Participants own optional post-interview feedback; Senger Advisory never sees the respondent identity or response text in the scorecard.
- The assigned advisor owns evidence review and POC closeout.
- Platform operations owns support-case review and cross-client pattern detection.
- Closeout should occur within two business days of the final debrief.

## Decision rules

- One difficult experience is a signal, not a shared-method change.
- Any confidentiality, access, data-integrity, or finding-release failure requires immediate review before another POC advances through the affected gate.
- Repeated friction across organizations should create a governed change candidate rather than an informal edit.
- “Ready to monetize” requires a completed diagnostic journey, a usable governed finding, a clear paid boundary, no unresolved critical incident, and an explicit cohort decision. Scores inform that judgment but never make it automatically.

## Closeout record

The advisor console must show which learning checkpoints were captured, the aggregate scorecard, cohort decision history, and the change ledger. Missing optional feedback remains visible as an open checkpoint but cannot block the client result.
