# Senger Advisory Brand System v0.2

## Position
Senger Advisory defines and operationalizes Organizational Capacity for growing companies.

## Tone
Clear, grounded, executive, and intelligent. Teach before selling. Confidence over hype.

## Color semantics
- Midnight navy (`#111c2e`): authority, depth, and the organizational system.
- Burnt orange (`#d45b31`): movement, consequence, attention, and decisive action.
- Charcoal navy (`#161c25`): primary editorial text and executive seriousness.
- Warm ivory (`#fffdf8`, `#f2efe8`): clarity, warmth, and editorial calm.
- Slate (`#505a67`, `#3e566e`, `#8fa5b8`): secondary information, healthy flow, and structural detail.
- Deep orange (`#9f3f22`): emphasis and high-contrast orange states.

This palette is intentionally shared with Senger Leadership. The two brands may
use different subject matter and visual metaphors, but should always read as one
brand family through midnight-navy and burnt-orange contrast, warm editorial
surfaces, and restrained slate support colors.

## Visual language
Flow, systems, constraints, signal, capacity, and movement. Avoid generic consulting imagery.
