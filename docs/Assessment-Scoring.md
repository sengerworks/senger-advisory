# Organizational Capacity Assessment — MVP Scoring v0.1

## Purpose

The MVP produces a directional view of organizational capacity across six domains. It is designed to support executive reflection and constraint hypotheses, not to claim normative comparison or psychometric validation.

## Structure

- Six domains: Leadership, Decision Velocity, Operating Rhythm, Alignment, Technology, and Culture.
- Three observable-behavior items per domain.
- Five response options from Rarely (1) to Consistently (5).
- Every item is positively keyed; higher responses indicate greater capacity.

## Calculation

1. Average the three item responses in each domain.
2. Normalize each domain to a 0–100 scale: `(average - 1) × 25`.
3. Average the six equally weighted domain scores.
4. Create a constraint-adjusted Organizational Capacity Index: `(domain mean × 0.75) + (lowest domain × 0.25)`.
5. Identify the lowest domain score as the primary constraint signal. Ties follow domain order and should be treated as co-occurring constraints in later report versions.

The weakest-domain adjustment reflects the model’s premise that a material constraint can affect execution across the system. The 75/25 weighting is an MVP product hypothesis and should be revisited with evidence.

## Interpretation bands

- 0–39: Constrained
- 40–59: Strained
- 60–79: Developing
- 80–100: Enabling

These bands are product hypotheses. They are not external benchmarks.

## Evidence roadmap

Before describing the assessment as validated, collect response distributions, internal consistency, test-retest behavior, domain correlations, outcome relationships, and qualitative case evidence. Thresholds and item wording should be revised when evidence warrants it.
