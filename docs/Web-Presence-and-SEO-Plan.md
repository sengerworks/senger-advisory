# Senger Advisory Web Presence and SEO Plan

## Positioning objective

Senger Advisory should be understood as the firm behind an Organizational Capacity Diagnostic—not as a general consulting practice. Search results, shared links, profiles, and third-party references should consistently connect the brand to organizational capacity, diagnostic evidence, decision velocity, operating friction, and governed intervention.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the current Organizational Capacity site to the primary production domain.
2. Confirm that `https://sengeradvisory.com/` serves the new homepage and that every canonical URL resolves without a redirect chain.
3. Confirm the legacy `/the-network` URL permanently redirects to the Platform Journey.
4. Add and verify both domain and URL-prefix properties in Google Search Console.
5. Submit `https://sengeradvisory.com/sitemap.xml` and request indexing for the homepage, Diagnostic, Platform Journey, Framework, About, and Insights pages.
6. Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
7. Align the Senger Advisory LinkedIn presence and Jonathan T. Senger's profile language with the website's Organizational Capacity positioning.

## Search architecture

The homepage owns the core brand and category association: **Senger Advisory Organizational Capacity Diagnostic**.

Supporting pages own distinct intent:

- Diagnostic: what the diagnostic reveals and how it works.
- Platform Journey: what an executive sponsor and participants experience.
- Framework: the Organizational Capacity model and its dimensions.
- Assessment: a lightweight indicator of likely organizational symptoms.
- Signs: recognizable execution-friction patterns.
- About: founder credibility, point of view, and firm identity.
- Insights: evidence-led thinking that earns topic authority over time.
- Contact: branded conversion intent.

Avoid repeating identical titles, descriptions, or primary headings across pages. Each page should answer one searcher's question completely and lead naturally to the diagnostic.

## Content authority plan

The current Insights page is a useful index, but durable organic visibility will require substantive, individually addressable articles. Build the first content cluster around questions executives already ask:

1. Why strategy stalls between leadership intent and organizational execution.
2. How to distinguish a people problem from an operating-system problem.
3. Why leadership surveys do not produce a defensible organizational diagnosis.
4. How anonymity thresholds improve the quality of executive evidence.
5. What decision velocity reveals about organizational capacity.
6. Why interventions fail when they target symptoms instead of system conditions.

Each article should have a stable URL, a distinct title and description, a clear author and review date, relevant internal links, and original examples or evidence. Publish for usefulness and credibility—not keyword volume.

## Trust and entity signals

- Use the same firm name, founder name, domain, description, and category language across the website, LinkedIn, directory profiles, podcast biographies, and partner references.
- Earn relevant links through executive publications, partner sites, association profiles, podcast appearances, and original research—not generic link directories.
- Keep privacy, confidentiality, and security explanations easy to find from both the diagnostic and conversion paths.
- Add client evidence only with permission and with enough context to be credible; avoid unsupported outcome claims.
- Consider a Google Business Profile only if Senger Advisory qualifies under Google's service-area or customer-facing business rules.

## Measurement

Track a small set of business-relevant signals monthly:

- branded search impressions and clicks for Senger Advisory;
- non-branded impressions for organizational-capacity and diagnostic themes;
- indexed pages and crawl errors;
- visits from relevant referring domains;
- assessment starts and completions;
- diagnostic inquiry conversions;
- Platform Journey engagement;
- qualified conversations attributable to organic search.

Search traffic alone is not the goal. The primary measure is whether the web presence creates qualified diagnostic conversations with the right executive sponsors.

## Ongoing governance

- Review Search Console coverage, queries, and enhancements monthly.
- Review titles and descriptions when search intent changes, not merely to chase rankings.
- Test the sitemap, canonical tags, structured data, crawler exclusions, and legacy redirects with every public-site release.
- Refresh high-value insights when the underlying thinking changes and show the revision date.
- Audit third-party brand descriptions quarterly for outdated “consulting” language.
