# Capacity Signal Assessment v2

## Product role

The Capacity Signal Assessment is a brief evidence-gathering instrument. It helps one respondent recognize possible execution demand, friction, Capacity Compensation, and gaps between formal operating architecture and lived behavior.

It does not measure Organizational Capacity or diagnose the organization.

## Experience

The respondent first answers “capacity for what?” by identifying what is changing the execution demand, what the organization must execute, the relevant time horizon, and optional organization-size and perspective context.

The 18 items then move through four evidence lenses:

1. Execution demand
2. Friction
3. Capacity Compensation
4. Formal and lived operating-system signals

The brief follows an executive narrative of **Demand → Emerging pattern → Decision risk → Disciplined next move**. It reports the execution context, demand dimensions that appear pronounced, a qualified friction-to-compensation-to-system-drift pathway, operating-system questions, competing explanations, balanced decision risk, one bounded observation action, and what the Diagnostic would need to investigate. The pathway is explicitly plausible rather than causal.

## Explicit non-outputs

The assessment does not produce an Organizational Capacity score, five mechanism scores, a primary constraint, a causal conclusion, a benchmark, or a prescribed intervention.

## Private saving

A respondent may save the interpreted Capacity Signal Brief without an account. The v2 profile is encrypted in the browser before storage, stores no individual item responses, requires the complete recovery link for decryption, expires after 12 months, is no-indexed, and remains incompatible with legacy six-domain profiles.

## Organizational aggregation contract

The minimized v2 submission contains only categorical demand and pattern identifiers. It excludes individual answers, identity, email, free-text execution context, scores, and constraint claims.

Below five submissions, every organizational signal is withheld. At or above five, aggregation may report categorical convergence—widespread or mixed—across complexity dimensions, friction, compensation, and operating signals. It does not average responses or declare a constraint.

The aggregation engine and contract are versioned foundations. Existing workspace collection rounds continue to use v1 until a separate migration moves workspace storage and views to the v2 submission contract.

## Legacy boundary

Assessment v1 remains available only for active workspace collection links created under the v1 contract and v1 encrypted recovery-link follow-ups. V1 and v2 results are not numerically or semantically compared. A legacy user begins a new v2 baseline.
