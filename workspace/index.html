Before you select

What you are trying to accomplish

You are designing the set of viewpoints needed to understand how this issue moves through the organization. The aim is not to include the most senior people or everyone affected. It is to include people who can see different parts of the same system.

Use this test for every perspective

Complete the sentence: “This perspective matters because they can see or experience something the others cannot.”

If two planned perspectives produce essentially the same answer, one may be redundant.
Illustration—not a required formula

For a cross-functional execution issue, five useful viewpoints might include:

  • the leader accountable for the decision;
  • a functional leader translating it into priorities;
  • a manager coordinating handoffs;
  • an individual contributor doing the work; and
  • a cross-functional peer receiving the output.
