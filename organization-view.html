    Shared Constraint Hypothesis

    Questions for the leadership team

      Closely aligned

      Perspectives cluster relatively near one another.

      Varied

      Leaders are observing meaningfully different conditions.

      Widely varied

      The spread itself warrants investigation before action.

      Perspective variation does not identify who is correct, prove organizational conflict, or establish statistical significance. It tells the team where shared inquiry may matter most.

      From View to Action
