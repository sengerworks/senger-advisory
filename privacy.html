Privacy

Privacy Policy

Senger Advisory collects only the information you choose to submit through the website.

Contact information

Information submitted through contact forms is used to respond to your inquiry and maintain relevant business records. It is not combined with anonymous assessment evidence.

Assessment evidence

The Organizational Capacity Assessment runs in your browser. Individual question responses are not transmitted or stored. If you explicitly choose to contribute anonymous evidence, Senger Advisory receives assessment lifecycle events, the six aggregate domain scores, the overall index and interpretation band, optional organizational context, and any accuracy rating or written feedback you choose to provide.

Each participating assessment is assigned a random session identifier so related anonymous events can be evaluated together. The evidence submission does not request your name, email address, organization name, or contact details. Assessment and scoring version numbers are retained so results can be interpreted against the method that produced them.

This evidence is used to evaluate completion patterns, improve questions and scoring hypotheses, and assess whether results reflect organizational experience. Event-level evidence is reviewed at least annually and deleted no later than 24 months after receipt unless it has already been converted into aggregate, de-identified research summaries.

Data practices

Senger Advisory does not sell personal information or anonymous assessment evidence. Reasonable technical controls are used to protect submitted information. This policy may be updated as the website and its services evolve.
