About

Senger Advisory exists because growing organizations lacked language for the constraint they could already feel.

The work sits at the intersection of leadership, organizational systems, execution, and capacity. It is built for companies that have momentum, but can feel the operating model straining underneath it.

Founder Lens

Twenty-five years building organizations.
Seven thousand hours coaching leaders.
One conclusion.

Organizations don’t outgrow strategy. They outgrow their capacity to execute it.

Point of View

Leadership matters. So does the system leaders are operating inside.

Senger Advisory helps executives see what is happening beneath visible symptoms: slow decisions, unclear ownership, meeting debt, change fatigue, and technology initiatives that do not translate into execution leverage.

The Capacity Lens makes the underlying system visible. Once leaders can see the conditions shaping how work moves, interventions become focused: leadership alignment, decision rights, operating rhythm, AI readiness, execution systems, and capacity expansion.
